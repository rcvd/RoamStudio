# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — 2026-11-28

- Roam's logo at the bottom of the left sidebar takes the wordmark's color in every theme and mode. Its white image vanished on the light sidebars of Blueprint and Gruvbox, and in Quattro, Craft and Things it was a different gray than the wordmark.
- **Phosphor** is a third icon theme, next to BlueprintJS and Lucide: softer, rounder icons whose weight follows the theme (light in Quattro and Things, regular in Craft, Blueprint and Gruvbox) and changes with it; the date icon follows too. Every icon keeps Roam's size and position, as with Lucide.
- The left sidebar's Desk icon follows the icon theme (Lucide and Phosphor); it kept BlueprintJS's.
- In Quattro, Craft and Things, Lucide icons in minimal buttons (the top bar's filter and calendar, the settings panel's group arrows) show again; the themes cleared their fill. The Lucide stylesheet is also smaller (187 → 139 KB): each icon's drawing is in it once.
- **Breaking:** the Feather icon theme is replaced by **Lucide**, Feather's maintained fork (Feather is no longer maintained). The icon theme offers BlueprintJS and Lucide; a Feather choice carries over and shows the Lucide icons, the date icon included. Lucide also replaces the icons Feather left as BlueprintJS's (callouts, dialogs, zoom controls, select carets; Roam's block carets stay), and no icon shows BlueprintJS's drawing cut or next to its replacement any more (the settings' Import icon, chevrons, close and help buttons); every icon keeps Roam's size and position.
- In Roam Depot's settings, Roam Studio's own buttons (Export, Import, Appearance, Gruvbox contrast) take the theme's button colors in Gruvbox, Quattro, Craft and Things. Roam paints buttons there dark even in Light, which those themes reproduced: Gruvbox Light showed dark buttons with unreadable text.
- In Quattro, a collapsed block's bullet has a ring, as in Craft and Things; it had looked exactly like an open bullet.
- **Collapsed bullets** (Blocks & Content) chooses how a collapsed block's bullet stands out in any theme: with a ring (as in Quattro, Craft and Things) or by intensity, with open bullets fainter (as in Blueprint and Gruvbox). Theme default keeps the theme's own.
- A link with an underline setting no longer gets Roam's text underline too when hovered (every theme), and links inside a block reference draw no underline of their own under the block reference's: both showed two lines.
- With the Feather icons, the magnifier of a page's references search sits centered in its field; it sat 4px too high.
- With **Enhanced search**, the search menu leaves room for Roam's footer with the keyboard shortcuts, which fell below the window in lower windows.
- In Craft, outlined buttons (Choose Folder in Roam's Import dialog, say) no longer gain a border on hover, which made them grow and shift their neighbors.
- In Craft, hovering a button (the tags of a page's or a reference's filter menu, say) no longer mutes its text, which in Dark turned darker on the lighter hover background; hover now shows only in the background, as in Blueprint.
- In Craft, tag and date pills stand out more: in Dark they are filled lighter than the page (their fill had matched it), and in Light a shade darker than before.
- In Craft and Things, the left sidebar's "Shortcuts" heading lines up with the shortcuts and the menu again, as in Quattro; it sat about 20px to the right.
- Quattro, Craft and Things show an active page or reference filter: its icon turns to the theme's accent (Quattro's cyan, Craft's blue, Things' link blue); before, it stayed gray.
- In Quattro, open bullets are as visible as collapsed ones. The faint bullets of the previous version are what **Subtle controls** does, which now fades bullets, multibars and reference carets until a block is hovered or focused, as in the previous version.
- In Quattro, Craft and Things, the magnifier of Find or Create Page sits centered in its field; it sat 2px too high.
- In Quattro, Craft and Things, the search of a page's references shows only its magnifier until it is opened, as in Blueprint.
- In Things' Dark mode, page links and done TODO boxes take the Things app's light blue (the boxes with a dark checkmark), and checked checkboxes, radios and switches a lighter blue too; they were too dark on the dark page.
- In Quattro, Craft and Things, buttons show their states as in Blueprint: hover, pressed and selected (the Appearance control in the settings shows the chosen mode), and disabled buttons are muted; hovered minimal buttons and select boxes change too, and in Things a focused input shows a frame. The settings' Export menu highlights the hovered row like every other menu.
- In Quattro, Craft and Things, the buttons of colored toasts are clear and take a tint of the toast's hue on hover, as in Blueprint, and toast text is readable on the light toasts (it was white on white on the plain toast and, in Light, on the primary toast).
- In a sidebar **References to** window, the highlighted reference row takes the theme's gray instead of Blueprint's blue gray, and in Dark a dark surface instead of a light one (every theme); the carets of its rows sit centered on the breadcrumb, as in a block's inline references.
- Clicking into the empty first block of a page ("Click here to start writing") no longer moves its bullet: it jumped 16px on daily notes in Blueprint and Gruvbox and 2px in Quattro, Craft and Things.
- In Quattro and Things, hovering a minimal button (the top bar's filter, say) no longer turns its icon white; on a light page it vanished.
- Quattro, Craft and Things paint selected blocks as the previous version did (its blue, or Craft's gray, in both modes), and the import dialog's text field keeps its code font and size.
- In Gruvbox's Dark mode, selected blocks are tinted in Gruvbox's blue, as in Light mode, and stand out in every contrast; before, the selection was barely visible, and invisible with hard contrast.
- Moving between blocks with the arrow keys is smooth again in every theme. Rules that recolored Roam's active autocomplete row, menu buttons and reference headers made every key press restyle most of the page; with them rewritten, a key press costs about 1–2 ms more than in Roam without the extension instead of up to 260 ms. The look is unchanged.
- In Quattro, Craft and Things, hovering a link no longer adds a text underline under the link's own underline: links keep their underline style on hover, as in the previous version.
- In Quattro, Craft and Things, the command palette's search icon is centered in its field again; it sat 7px too high.
- With the Feather icons, the Advanced Search field's search icon is drawn cleanly too.
- Settings can be exported and imported: **Export** at the top of the settings downloads a `.json` file or copies the text, **Import** reads such a file or pasted text, shows what changes and replaces the whole customization after **Apply**. Unknown settings and invalid values are skipped and listed.
- Roam Studio is rebuilt on the Blueprint theme engine: generated light and dark rules, consistent Auto, Dark and Light modes, and the Blueprint and Gruvbox themes.
- Quattro is back as the default theme, rebuilt on the engine: its look is reproduced from the previous version and checked against it element by element. It costs about as much as Blueprint: a full style and layout recalculation of Roam's Change Log page takes 43.9 ms with Quattro and 43.8 ms with Blueprint on a light system, 44.3 and 42.0 ms on a dark one (medians measured in one session, 2026-11-22).
- Craft and Things are back too, reproduced from the previous version in the same way. Their tag and date pills now have the 15px radius the previous version intended (it rendered them square), and Things fills in colors the previous version referred to but never defined. Measured in the same way, Craft takes 43.8 ms (light) and 44.7 ms (dark), Things 44.5 and 44.6 ms, against Blueprint's 40.2 and 41.1 ms.
- Tags and date links have an underline color setting.
- The emoji picker (block reactions) follows the theme and the Dark/Light setting; before, it followed only the system. Diagram dot grids, slash-command icons, the autocomplete's hint, kanban cards in dark mode and callouts inside link previews take the theme's colors too.
- Quattro, Craft and Things style All Pages, toasts, the slider and the graph view's controls as the previous version did.
- Quattro, Craft and Things highlight the hovered and the selected row of every menu (Roam's search, context menus, the autocomplete and the others) in the theme's own menu color, which only their BlueprintJS menus used before. The command palette keeps the previous version's active row; in Quattro and Things its white text had been left on the light menu color.
- In every theme, autocomplete rows highlight on hover like a menu row.
- Quattro, Craft and Things center menu icons on their row; they sat about 2px below the text.
- Quattro, Craft and Things round the top corners of the command palette's search field like the palette, so its border and focus frame are no longer cut off at the corners.
- Quattro, Craft and Things match the previous version in more places: the block menu's text-alignment buttons (flat, the active one in the accent color), the disabled zoom-out button of the image zoom, the date and time pickers, the search menu's match highlight, switch knobs in dark mode, the mentions search, the submenu arrow, the SmartBlocks menu's active row, the select buttons and text inputs in Roam's settings, and, in Craft and Things, the muted labels of forms in dark mode. Menu items in a bold context no longer turn bold.
- Roam's settings dialog looks as in the previous version again in Quattro, Craft and Things: the selected tab in the theme's gray instead of BlueprintJS's blue, rounded tabs, no separator lines between sections, the previous spacing of the panel and its heading, single frames and the smaller text size on the password and number fields too, sign-in providers framed in the theme's border color, the account email without underline, links in descriptions in the theme's link style, outlined radio buttons in Quattro, and, in Craft and Things, muted switch and radio labels in dark mode.
- Text in Roam's settings dialog is readable in every theme and mode again. The graph size table and the time-travel field's button had text in the dark mode's color on the light dialog (a generator fault styled every element with Roam's `bp3-dark` class). Active rows of menus keep their text readable on the theme's active color (Craft and Things drew white on light gray in all menus, Quattro white on its cyan, which in dark mode is now a dark teal); in Things, hovered rows and the rows' secondary labels are readable in light mode too. In Quattro, Craft and Things the warning, success and danger buttons use shades that reach 4.5:1, and the success button is green in Craft and Things instead of the danger red; descriptions, Things' minimal buttons and Quattro's selected tab in dark mode reach 4.5:1 too.
- Quattro, Craft and Things style the help popup, the extension marketplace, block quotes, dividers and the agent chat's headings as the previous version did; the help popup's colors no longer turn white in dark mode in Craft and Things.
- The checkmark of a checked TODO sits centered in its box again in Quattro, Craft and Things (in Craft it sat at the right edge), with the box sizes of the previous version.
- In Craft and Things, the left sidebar's menu and shortcuts line up with the graph name again.
- With the Feather icons, the search icons of the top bar's search field, of buttons and of the agent chat, and the import icon, are drawn cleanly; the Blueprint icon underneath had cut into them.
- In Quattro, Craft and Things, the left menu of Roam's settings dialog sits flush at the dialog's edge with its own background and a fine separator, as in the previous version; a strip in another shade showed beside it.
- Quattro, Craft and Things also take the previous version's shapes and spacing again where it had its own: flat checkboxes and buttons without BlueprintJS's gradient, menu rows centered at the previous line height, block references without extra padding, full-opacity reference counts, the centered TODO checkmark, date picker cells, single frames on the text inputs and select buttons in Roam's settings, and the top bar search field's focus ring.
- Quattro and Things draw the icons of the top bar's help, more and layout buttons in the same color as the calendar and filter icons, as the previous version did; in light mode they were white on white.
- In Craft and Things, the heading size, color and font, the base font size, the code font and code size settings, and the date link settings (color, background, underline, size, corner radius) now apply; theme rules had kept their own values over the settings.
- The "More room" module no longer hides the bullet of a block that holds an empty block further down, such as a query with an empty result block.
- Moving through blocks with the arrow keys restyles less: embedded tweets and Pomodoro timers no longer make every block check its content.
- **Breaking:** settings now use their own keys (`rs-…`). Settings of the previous Roam Studio version are not carried over; every setting starts at its theme default.
